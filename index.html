About ROGBOLT & Our Origin

Built on permanence, structural strength, and step-by-step execution.

Kjeragbolten → Ragbolt → ROGBOLT

Anchored like Kjeragbolten • Engineered like a Ragbolt

Our name draws inspiration from two symbols of unshakable stability: Kjeragbolten, the massive glacial boulder wedged immovably between two towering mountain cliffs in Norway, and the industrial Ragbolt — a heavy-duty anchor bolt engineered to lock critical structures permanently into solid rock. ROGBOLT carries that exact engineering philosophy into the digital world: building rock-solid IT and cloud foundations that businesses can anchor their growth on with complete confidence.

Our Vision & Current Stage

ROGBOLT is a technology venture founded with a long-term vision to grow into a comprehensive B2B IT and Cloud services partner. Today, we focus on delivering select web, cloud setup, and IT consulting engagements with high accountability, expanding our capabilities progressively as our team and partnerships grow.
